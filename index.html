What we believe

Build boldly and responsibly.

Spark AI teaches students how to bring their ideas to life using AI, while developing creativity, critical thinking, technical skills, and responsible habits.

01

Creativity

Students are encouraged to explore, experiment, and turn their own ideas into something they can create, test, and share using programming and AI.

02

Technical Skills

Students learn how AI works while building foundational skills in programming, logic, and design to create their own games, applications, websites, and digital projects.

03

Critical Thinking

Students learn to evaluate the tools and information they use, with AI supporting their ideas instead of replacing them.

04

Safety & Responsibility

Students develop safe and responsible technology habits, from protecting their privacy and making informed choices to understanding how and when to use AI effectively.

For parents

Safety is built in, not bolted on.

Every one of the 8 sessions includes a dedicated digital-safety lesson. These aren't afterthoughts. They're core curriculum.

Supervised program accounts only

Students never connect personal email, social media, or existing accounts. Every tool runs through program-managed accounts set up by the instructor.

No personal information in AI tools

Students are explicitly taught never to enter their real name, address, school, or any identifying information into AI tools or their projects.

Parental permission required

Any use of information that could be traced back to a student requires explicit written parent permission. A firm program rule, not a suggestion.

Filtered tools, monitored sessions

All AI tools have robust content filters, and the instructor monitors student interactions with AI tools throughout every session.

Questions

Parents ask us

Does my child need coding experience?

No. The program is designed for complete beginners. AI tools handle the heavy lifting of syntax so students can focus on ideas, logic, and creativity. Experienced coders are challenged too: we push them toward more ambitious projects.

What will my child actually build?

Real, working projects they can access via a URL and show anyone. Students have built personal websites, quiz games, weather apps, homework trackers, and tools to help organize family events. Every student leaves every session with something that works. See a real example →

How is this different from other coding programs?

Most coding programs teach kids to follow tutorials or memorize syntax. We teach kids to identify problems, imagine solutions, and use AI as a building tool to bring those solutions to life. The real skill is creative problem-solving, clear communication, and iterative thinking.

What device does my child need?

Any laptop with a web browser and internet access. Chromebooks work great, and we can provide loaners if needed.

Can I observe a session?

Absolutely. Parents are welcome to sit in, especially for Session 8, Demo Day, which is designed as a showcase for families and guests.

What happens after the 8 weeks?

We're planning a summer camp extension (a full-week intensive) for students who want to go deeper, plus resources for continued independent building and possible advanced sessions in the fall.
